# Nimbus — Hot Drink Recipes

These are the build sheets Nimbus answers from. They are an approximation of
standard Starbucks builds, written as a teaching fixture for this workshop. They
are not official Starbucks specifications, and stores vary. Nothing before the
first section heading is indexed.

## Caffè Latte
Espresso with steamed milk and a thin 1 cm layer of foam. Grande is 2 shots and milk to the top, finished with foam. Tall is 1 shot, Venti hot is 2 shots, Venti iced is 3. No syrup unless the order asks for one. The default milk is 2% unless another is requested.

## Cappuccino
The same shots as a latte, but roughly half steamed milk and half foam, so the cup is lighter and the coffee reads stronger. Grande is 2 shots. Foam should be about 2 cm deep. Never made with less foam on request; a low-foam cappuccino is a latte.

## Flat White
Built on ristretto shots rather than full shots, with whole milk steamed to a thin microfoam and a dot poured on top. Tall is 2 ristretto, Grande is 3 ristretto, Venti is 4. Ristretto is a shorter pull, so it tastes sweeter and less bitter than a standard shot.

## Caffè Americano
Espresso topped with hot water, which changes the strength but removes nothing, so the caffeine is the same as the shots it holds. Shots run one higher than a latte at every size: Tall 2, Grande 3, Venti hot 4. Water goes in first, shots on top, so the crema survives.

## Caffè Mocha
Mocha sauce, espresso, steamed milk, and whipped cream on top. Grande is 3 pumps of mocha sauce and 2 shots. The mocha sauce contains dairy, so a mocha cannot be made fully dairy free even with an alternative milk. Whipped cream is standard and can be left off.

## White Chocolate Mocha
White chocolate mocha sauce, espresso, steamed milk, whipped cream. Grande is 3 pumps and 2 shots. The white chocolate sauce contains dairy and is the only sauce that cannot be substituted, so this drink is never dairy free.

## Caramel Macchiato
Built upside down and never stirred: vanilla syrup first, then steamed milk, then the shots poured through the top, finished with a caramel drizzle crosshatch. Grande is 4 pumps of vanilla and 2 shots. The layering is the drink; stirring it makes it a vanilla latte with caramel.

## Cinnamon Dolce Latte
Cinnamon dolce syrup, espresso, steamed milk, whipped cream and a cinnamon dolce topping. Grande is 4 pumps and 2 shots. Without the whipped cream and topping it is simply a cinnamon dolce latte.

## Caffè Misto
Half brewed coffee, half steamed milk, no espresso at all. Grande is 8 oz of drip and 8 oz of steamed milk. It is the cheapest milk drink on the menu because it uses no shots, and it is the usual answer for a customer who wants a milky coffee without espresso.

## Espresso Macchiato
A double shot marked with a small dollop of foam, served in a demitasse. Nothing else goes in it. It shares a name with the caramel macchiato and is a completely different drink, which is the most common ordering mix-up on the bar.

## Espresso Con Panna
A double shot topped with whipped cream, served short. No milk, no syrup.

## Chai Tea Latte
Chai concentrate with steamed milk, no espresso and no brewed tea. Concentrate is pumped like a syrup: Tall 3, Grande 4, Venti hot 5, Venti iced 6. Adding a shot makes it a Dirty Chai. The concentrate itself contains no dairy, so it is dairy free with an alternative milk.

## Matcha Tea Latte
Matcha blend whisked with steamed milk. Scoops rather than pumps: Tall 2, Grande 3, Venti hot 4, Venti iced 4. The matcha blend is pre-sweetened with sugar, so an unsweetened matcha latte is not possible.

## Hot Chocolate
Mocha sauce and vanilla syrup with steamed milk and whipped cream, no coffee. Grande is 3 pumps mocha and 2 pumps vanilla. A Peppermint Hot Chocolate is the same build with peppermint syrup replacing the vanilla.

## Steamed Milk
Milk steamed to serving temperature with no coffee and no flavour, sometimes ordered as a Steamer with syrup added. It is the standard order for a child, and the default steaming temperature is lower than for espresso drinks.

## Brewed Coffee
Batch brewed and held for 30 minutes before it is discarded. A Grande contains roughly 310 mg of caffeine, which is more than any two-shot espresso drink on this menu. Refills are free for a customer drinking in with a registered account.

## Caffeine by drink
A single shot of espresso has about 75 mg. A Grande latte, with 2 shots, has about 150 mg. A Grande brewed coffee has about 310 mg. A Grande cold brew has about 205 mg. Decaf espresso has about 3 mg per shot, which is not zero.

## Decaf and half-caf
Any espresso drink can be made decaf at no extra charge, and half-caf splits the shots between decaf and regular. Brewed decaf is only guaranteed before 12:00 because it is batch brewed. Cold brew is never available decaf; it is steeped overnight in a single batch.
