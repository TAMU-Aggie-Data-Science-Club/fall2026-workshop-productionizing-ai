# Nimbus — Build Standards

The rules that apply across every recipe. Approximate standard builds, written
as a teaching fixture for this workshop. Not official Starbucks specifications.

## Cup sizes
Short is 8 oz, Tall is 12 oz, Grande is 16 oz, Venti hot is 20 oz and Venti iced is 24 oz. Venti iced is larger than Venti hot, which is why iced drinks take an extra pump at that size. Trenta is 30 oz and is only used for refreshers, iced tea, iced coffee and cold brew.

## Shot standards
Short and Tall take 1 shot, Grande 2, Venti hot 2 and Venti iced 3. Americano and flat white are the exceptions and are listed on their own recipes. An extra shot can be added to any drink for an additional charge.

## Syrup pump standards
Tall 3 pumps, Grande 4, Venti hot 5, Venti iced 6. This applies to every clear syrup, including vanilla, caramel, hazelnut, cinnamon dolce, brown sugar and chai concentrate.

## Sauce pump standards
Sauces are always one pump fewer than syrups at the same size: Tall 2, Grande 3, Venti hot 4, Venti iced 5. Mocha, white chocolate mocha and dark caramel are sauces, not syrups. Caramel drizzle is a topping and is not pumped at all.

## Milk options
2% is the default in every hot espresso drink. Whole, nonfat, breve, oatmilk, soymilk, almondmilk and coconutmilk are all available. Alternative milks carry no upcharge. Whole milk is the default only in the flat white, where the texture depends on it.

## What is not dairy free
Mocha sauce, white chocolate mocha sauce, vanilla sweet cream, whipped cream, Frappuccino chips and the caramel drizzle all contain dairy. Substituting the milk does not make a drink containing any of these dairy free. Chai concentrate, matcha blend and every refresher base contain no dairy.

## Allergens and shared equipment
Every steaming wand, blender pitcher and shaker is shared across drinks and is rinsed rather than sanitised between orders. Nimbus states what the notes say about an ingredient and never promises that a drink is free of an allergen. A customer with a severe allergy is told to speak to the barista, because the notes cannot settle it.

## Frappuccino base
Every blended Frappuccino needs a base to hold the texture: Frappuccino roast for coffee versions, crème base for the rest. Base pumps run Tall 2, Grande 3, Venti 4. Without base the drink separates within a minute, which is the most common build error on the bar.

## Blonde and Signature espresso
Signature Espresso is the darker default. Blonde Espresso is lighter, smoother and slightly higher in caffeine at about 85 mg per shot against 75 mg. Any espresso drink can be switched to Blonde at no charge. The Brown Sugar Oatmilk Shaken Espresso is built with Blonde by default.

## Ristretto and long shot
A ristretto is pulled shorter than a standard shot, giving a sweeter and less bitter cup, and it is the default in the flat white. A long shot is pulled longer and tastes more bitter. Both use the same amount of coffee, so the caffeine is roughly unchanged.

## Whipped cream
Standard on the mocha, white chocolate mocha, hot chocolate, espresso con panna and every Frappuccino. It contains dairy and can be left off any drink on request. Leaving it off does not change any other part of the build.

## Ordering sequence
Say the size, then hot or iced, then any milk substitution, then the drink, then modifications. Given in that order a barista can write the cup in one pass. It is the order the cup itself is printed in, which is why it is the order the bar expects.

## Customisation limits
Syrup pumps can be adjusted up or down freely. Shots can be added but not reduced below one. A cappuccino cannot be made with less foam, a flat white cannot be made with standard shots, and a caramel macchiato cannot be stirred — in each case the change would make it a different drink with a different name.

## Temperature and texture
Milk is steamed to 71 °C by default and to 82 °C for an extra-hot order. Above 82 °C the proteins scorch and the milk will not hold foam, so extra-hot is the highest temperature the bar will make. Kids' temperature is 54 °C.
