# Nimbus — Cold Drink Recipes

Approximate standard builds, written as a teaching fixture for this workshop.
Not official Starbucks specifications.

## Iced Caffè Latte
Shots poured over ice with cold milk, no foam. Grande is 2 shots, Venti iced is 3. Iced drinks take one more pump of syrup than their hot counterparts at Venti, because the cup is 24 oz rather than 20 oz.

## Iced Caramel Macchiato
Vanilla syrup and cold milk over ice, shots poured through the top, caramel drizzle on top. Grande is 4 pumps and 2 shots. Like the hot version it is layered and served unstirred, so the first sip is milk and the last is espresso.

## Iced Shaken Espresso
Shots, syrup and ice shaken together, then topped with a splash of milk. Shaking chills and aerates the shots, which is what gives the drink its foam collar. Grande is 2 shots and 4 pumps.

## Iced Brown Sugar Oatmilk Shaken Espresso
Blonde espresso, brown sugar syrup and cinnamon shaken with ice, topped with oatmilk. Grande is 2 shots and 4 pumps. It is built with oatmilk by default, so it is one of the few drinks that is dairy free as it is written.

## Cold Brew
Coarse ground coffee steeped in cold water for 20 hours, then filtered. Never brewed hot and never made to order. A Grande has about 205 mg of caffeine. Served black over ice unless the order adds something.

## Vanilla Sweet Cream Cold Brew
Cold brew with vanilla syrup, topped with vanilla sweet cream that is poured last so it cascades through the cup. Grande is 3 pumps of vanilla and 2 oz of sweet cream. The sweet cream is made with heavy cream and cannot be made dairy free.

## Nitro Cold Brew
Cold brew infused with nitrogen and poured from a tap, which gives it a cascading head and a naturally sweet texture with no sugar added. It is served without ice, because ice would flatten the foam, and it is not available in Venti.

## Caramel Frappuccino
Coffee Frappuccino syrup, brewed Frappuccino roast, milk, ice and caramel syrup, blended and topped with whipped cream and caramel drizzle. Grande is 3 pumps of Frappuccino base and 2 pumps of caramel syrup. Every Frappuccino is blended to order.

## Java Chip Frappuccino
The Caramel Frappuccino build with mocha sauce and Frappuccino chips blended in, topped with whipped cream and mocha drizzle. Grande is 3 pumps mocha sauce and 2 scoops of chips. The chips contain dairy.

## Crème Frappuccino
A Frappuccino made with the crème base instead of Frappuccino roast, so it contains no coffee at all. Vanilla Bean and Strawberry are the standing crème options and both are ordered most often for children.

## Strawberry Açaí Refresher
Strawberry açaí base shaken with ice and freeze-dried strawberries. It contains green coffee extract, so it is caffeinated at about 45 mg per Grande even though it does not taste of coffee. This surprises customers who order it as a coffee-free drink.

## Pink Drink
A Strawberry Açaí Refresher made with coconutmilk instead of water. That single substitution is the whole drink. It is dairy free as written, because coconutmilk is the default rather than an upgrade.

## Mango Dragonfruit Refresher
Mango dragonfruit base shaken with ice and freeze-dried dragonfruit pieces. Made with coconutmilk it becomes a Dragon Drink. Like every refresher it contains green coffee extract.

## Iced Chai Tea Latte
Chai concentrate and cold milk over ice, no espresso. Grande is 4 pumps, Venti iced is 6. Adding shots makes it a Dirty Chai.

## Iced Matcha Tea Latte
Matcha blend shaken with cold milk over ice. Grande is 3 scoops, Venti iced is 4. The matcha blend is pre-sweetened.

## Iced Tea
Black, green or passion tango tea shaken with ice and, by default, liquid cane sugar: Tall 3 pumps, Grande 4, Venti iced 6. Ordering it unsweetened is the most common modification on the menu. A Half Tea Half Lemonade replaces half the water with lemonade.

## Iced Coffee
Brewed double strength, cooled, and served over ice, which is not the same drink as cold brew. It is sweetened by default with liquid cane sugar and has about 165 mg of caffeine in a Grande.

## Espresso Frappuccino
A Frappuccino built with shots as well as Frappuccino roast, for a customer who wants the blended texture at full espresso strength. Grande is 2 shots on top of the standard base.
