# Rev's Brew — Drinks

## Espresso
A single shot is 7 grams of ground coffee pulled for 25 to 30 seconds, yielding about 30 ml. A double is 14 grams and about 60 ml. Every espresso drink on the menu is built on a double unless you ask otherwise.

## Americano
A double espresso topped with 180 ml of hot water. It tastes lighter than espresso but carries the same caffeine, because nothing is removed. Served in a 12 oz cup by default. $3.25.

## Latte
A double espresso with 240 ml of steamed milk and a thin layer of foam. The mildest espresso drink we make. $4.50 in 12 oz, $5.00 in 16 oz. Any milk substitution is free.

## Cappuccino
A double espresso with 120 ml of steamed milk and 120 ml of foam, so it is half the milk of a latte in the same cup. Stronger coffee flavour. Only made in 12 oz. $4.25.

## Flat white
A double ristretto with 160 ml of milk steamed to a thinner texture than a latte. Served in 8 oz only, so it is the most concentrated milk drink we make. $4.75.

## Mocha
A double espresso, 200 ml steamed milk, and 30 ml of dark chocolate sauce. The chocolate sauce contains dairy, so a mocha cannot be made fully dairy free. $5.25.

## Cortado
A double espresso cut with 60 ml of steamed milk, served in a 4 oz glass. Equal parts coffee and milk. $4.00.

## Drip coffee
Brewed in batches every 45 minutes from our house blend. A 12 oz cup contains roughly 180 mg of caffeine, more than a double espresso. $2.50, and refills are $1.00 all day.

## Cold brew
Coarse grounds steeped in cold water for 18 hours, then filtered. Lower acidity than drip and about 200 mg of caffeine per 12 oz. Never made with hot water. $4.00.

## Iced latte
A double espresso poured over ice with 240 ml of cold milk. Not the same as cold brew with milk, which is steeped rather than pulled. $4.75.

## Caffeine by drink
A double espresso has about 130 mg. A 12 oz drip coffee has about 180 mg. A 12 oz cold brew has about 200 mg. Decaf espresso has about 5 mg, not zero.

## Decaf
Any espresso drink can be made with decaf beans at no extra charge. Drip coffee is decaf only after 18:00. Cold brew is never available decaf, because it is steeped in a single overnight batch.

## Tea
Loose leaf, steeped to order. English breakfast, earl grey, jasmine green, peppermint and rooibos. Peppermint and rooibos are naturally caffeine free. $3.00 for any size.

## Chai latte
A spiced black tea concentrate with steamed milk, 120 ml of each. Contains caffeine. Our chai concentrate contains no dairy, so it can be made fully dairy free with an alternative milk. $4.50.

## Matcha latte
Ceremonial grade matcha whisked with 240 ml of steamed milk. About 70 mg of caffeine, less than a double espresso. Naturally dairy free before the milk is added. $5.00.

## Hot chocolate
Steamed milk with 40 ml of the same dark chocolate sauce used in the mocha. Contains dairy from the sauce. Caffeine free apart from trace amounts in the chocolate. $4.00.

## Seasonal drinks
The autumn menu is a maple latte and a spiced apple tea. Seasonal drinks rotate every three months and are not available outside their season. The maple syrup is dairy free.

## Sizes
We pour 8, 12 and 16 oz. Cappuccino and flat white are fixed sizes and cannot be scaled up, because the milk to coffee ratio is what defines them. Every other drink is available in all three.

## Extra shots and syrups
An extra espresso shot is $1.00 and adds about 65 mg of caffeine. Syrups are $0.50 each: vanilla, caramel, hazelnut and maple. All four syrups are dairy free; the caramel sauce, which is different from caramel syrup, is not.

## Bring your own cup
Bring any clean cup and take $0.25 off any drink. We will not fill a cup larger than 16 oz. This discount stacks with the loyalty card.
