# Rev's Brew — Cafe information

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday 07:00 to 20:00. Saturday 08:00 to 18:00. Sunday 09:00 to 16:00. The last coffee order is taken fifteen minutes before closing on every day.

## Exam week hours
During the two weeks of finals we stay open until 23:00 Monday to Friday. Hot food still stops at 15:00; only pastries and drinks are served in the extended hours.

## Location
Ground floor of the Evans library annexe, on the north side facing the quad. The nearest entrance is the one beside the bike racks, not the main library doors.

## Mobile ordering
Order through the campus app up to thirty minutes ahead. Mobile orders are made in the same queue as walk ups, so a busy counter delays them too. Pickup is the shelf by the window.

## Payment
We take card, campus dining dollars and the app. We stopped accepting cash in 2024. Dining dollars cover food and drink but not merchandise or beans.

## Loyalty card
Every tenth drink is free. The card is scanned in the app; there is no paper card. The free drink can be any size but does not cover extra shots or syrups.

## Student discount
Ten percent off with a student ID, all day, on drinks and food. It does not apply to beans, merchandise or catering, and it does not stack with the half price evening pastries.

## Wifi
The network is the campus wifi; there is no separate cafe network. If your device cannot see it, the library help desk one floor up handles wifi problems, not the cafe staff.

## Seating
Forty seats, including eight at the bar and four outside. We do not reserve tables. During finals a two hour limit applies at the large tables when every seat is taken.

## Study rules
Conversation is fine anywhere. The four bar seats by the window are the quiet seats. We do not have private rooms; the library upstairs takes bookings for those.

## Lost property
Anything left behind is kept behind the counter for seven days, then handed to the campus lost property office in the student union. Laptops and phones go there the same day.

## Buying beans
We sell our house blend and the single origin by the 250 gram bag. We grind to order and will grind a bag you bought elsewhere for free. Beans are not covered by dining dollars.

## Catering
We take orders for boxed coffee and pastry platters with two working days notice. Minimum order is twelve people. Email the cafe rather than ordering through the app.

## Feedback
Feedback goes to the manager through the app or the card by the till. We do not handle complaints about the library building, its opening hours or its printers.
