# Rev's Brew — Food and allergens

## Milk options
We stock whole, skim, oat, almond and soy. Oat, almond and soy are free substitutions on any drink. Oat steams closest to whole milk and is what we use by default for latte art.

## Nut allergy warning
Almond milk is stored and steamed with shared equipment. We rinse wands between drinks but we cannot guarantee a nut free drink. Tell the barista and we will use a dedicated backup pitcher.

## Gluten
The only reliably gluten free items are the flourless chocolate cake and the fruit cup. Everything else is baked in a kitchen that handles wheat daily, including items that contain no wheat themselves.

## Croissant
Butter laminated, baked each morning at 05:30. Contains wheat, dairy and egg. Sells out most days before 10:00. $3.50, or $4.75 filled with ham and gruyere.

## Almond croissant
A day old croissant soaked in syrup, filled with frangipane and baked again. Contains wheat, dairy, egg and almonds. This is the only pastry with nuts baked into it. $4.25.

## Blueberry muffin
Contains wheat, dairy and egg. Made with fresh blueberries, not preserves. One of the two vegetarian breakfast items that keeps past midday. $3.25.

## Banana bread
Contains wheat and egg but no dairy, because it is made with oil rather than butter. Toasted on request, which uses the shared grill. $3.50.

## Flourless chocolate cake
Contains dairy and egg, no wheat. Baked in a separate pan and stored on its own shelf, so it is our safest option for a coeliac customer. $4.50.

## Bagel
Plain, sesame or everything. Contains wheat. Sesame and everything both contain sesame seeds. Served with cream cheese, or with butter on request. $2.75 plain, $4.00 with cream cheese.

## Avocado toast
Sourdough, smashed avocado, chilli flakes and lime. Contains wheat. Vegan as served. Add a poached egg for $2.00, which makes it vegetarian rather than vegan. $7.50.

## Turkey and swiss sandwich
Sourdough, roast turkey, swiss cheese, lettuce and mustard. Contains wheat and dairy. Served cold unless you ask for it pressed. $8.50.

## Hummus wrap
Spinach tortilla, hummus, cucumber, roasted pepper and rocket. Contains wheat and sesame, from the tahini in the hummus. Vegan. $7.75.

## Soup of the day
Rotates daily and is posted on the board by 11:00. Monday is always tomato basil, which is vegan. The other days may contain dairy or chicken stock; ask before ordering. $6.00 with bread.

## Fruit cup
Seasonal fruit, cut each morning. No wheat, dairy, egg or nuts. Vegan and gluten free. The only item that is safe on every allergy we track. $4.00.

## Vegan items
Banana bread is not vegan, because it contains egg. The vegan items are avocado toast without the egg, the hummus wrap, the fruit cup, and Monday's tomato basil soup.

## Breakfast hours
Hot breakfast is served until 11:00. Pastries stay out all day until they sell out. The grill is switched off at 15:00, so nothing can be pressed or toasted after that.

## Ordering food
Food is made to order except pastries and the fruit cup. Expect eight to twelve minutes at peak. We will call your name; we do not bring food to tables.

## Leftover pastries
Anything unsold after 17:00 is half price. After 19:00 it goes to the campus food pantry rather than being held overnight, so late evening pastry stock is unpredictable.
